My Self

I’m Anthony Febrian Aria Sena, self-proclaimed engineer and your friendly neighborhood IT guy based in Jakarta.

This all started when my parents bought me a computer. I’ve been poking at machines to see what happens ever since. I firmly believe the fastest way to master anything is to get your hands dirty: touch it until it breaks, figure out how to fix it, and repeat.

I’m mostly powered by overly complicated side quests, obscure rabbit holes, and late-night meme scrolling.

By day, I get paid to stare at network traffic and hunt down cyber threats—because breaking and fixing things legally is far more sustainable than doing it for free.

Outside the 9-to-5, I build and maintain niche tools to help with my work, write Capture the Flag (CTF) challenges (mostly to watch people suffer), and contribute to the cybersecurity community as a developer, editor, and occasional speaker. Previously, I spent a few years teaching computer science until they handed me a degree and politely asked me to leave.
